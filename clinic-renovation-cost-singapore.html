Cost of Clinic Renovation in Singapore: What Affects the Quotation

Clinic Renovation

The cost of a clinic renovation in Singapore cannot be given as one estimate, because every clinic has a different size and layout. These are the main factors that affect the cost, so you can prepare before you ask for a quotation.

Clinic Size and Layout

The bigger the clinic, the more materials and labour the project needs. The layout is as important as the size. A clinic with several consultation rooms and treatment rooms is a more complex project, even when the floor area is similar. Send your floor plan early.

Scope of Works

Not every clinic needs a full renovation. Some need only a partial renovation, such as the waiting area or a few rooms. A full fit-out covers all works, from the first layout discussion to handover. A smaller scope means a lower cost.

Materials and Finishes

Your choice of flooring, wall finishes, laminates and other materials affects the cost. Clinics usually choose materials that are easy to clean. Ask for a detailed quotation that lists all materials.

Carpentry

Reception counters, cabinets and storage are part of most clinic projects. Custom carpentry made to fit your rooms suits a clinic better than standard furniture. When the carpentry is built in-house, your contractor keeps it part of the same renovation plan.

X-Ray Room Construction

If your dental or medical clinic needs an X-ray room, include it in the project from the start. X-ray room construction changes the scope of works, so tell your contractor at the first meeting.

How to Get a Clear Quotation

  1. Send us your floor plan.
  2. List the rooms you need: consultation rooms, treatment rooms, waiting area, X-ray room.
  3. Tell us the materials and finishes you prefer.
  4. Ask for a quotation that lists the scope of works and the materials.
